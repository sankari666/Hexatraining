1.	What is Sprint planning meeting?
Sprint planning is a collaborative Agile event where the team determines the highest-priority work 
to be completed in the upcoming cycle (usually 2–4 weeks). Guided by a single overarching Sprint Goal, 
the team forecasts the specific backlog items they can realistically deliver based on their capacity

2.	What is retrospective meeting?
An Agile retrospective (or "retro") is a recurring meeting held at the end of an iteration (typically a 2-to-4 week "sprint"). 
Its purpose is to help the team reflect on their recent work, identify processes or dynamics to improve, and agree on actionable changes for the next cycle.

3.	What is daily standup meeting?
A daily stand-up (or Daily Scrum) is a quick, 15-minute daily sync for agile teams. 
Its goal is to align on sprint progress, coordinate tasks for the next 24 hours, and flag any roadblocks—not to act as a lengthy status report
