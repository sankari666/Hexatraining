1.	What is Devops?

DevOps is a cultural philosophy and set of technical practices that unifies software development (Dev) and IT operations (Ops). 
It breaks down traditional silos to automate and accelerate the software delivery lifecycle, enabling teams to release applications 
and updates faster, more reliably, and with higher quality.

2.	Advantages of devops

DevOps bridges software development and IT operations to accelerate delivery, reduce costs, and improve software quality through 
continuous automation, rapid feedback loops, and cross-team collaboration.
•	Faster time to delivery
•	Rapid feedback loop
•	Rapid recovery
•	Enhanced collaboration
•	Shared ownership
•	Increased innovation

3.	What is agile methodology and what is scrum?

Agile is a broad project management philosophy that emphasizes flexibility, iterative progress, and continuous collaboration. 
Scrum is one specific framework used to implement Agile principles, breaking work down into structured, time-boxed cycles called 
"sprints" to deliver incremental value.

4.	Importance of meetings in agile

Meetings in Agile (often called ceremonies) drive continuous collaboration, alignment, and adaptability. They ensure teams deliver 
iterative value, resolve blockers quickly, and consistently adapt the product to changing client needs.

5.	Advantages of agile
Agile is an iterative project management approach that breaks work into smaller, manageable increments. Its core advantages 
include faster time-to-market, high adaptability to change, continuous customer involvement, and superior product quality through ongoing testing and feedback.
•  Adaptability & Flexibility: Teams can easily pivot and adjust priorities based on real-time market conditions 
or late-stage user feedback without disrupting the entire project. 
•  Faster Delivery: By breaking projects into short, focused cycles (sprints), valuable features are released continuously
rather than waiting for a massive final launch
•  Enhanced Customer Satisfaction: Clients and end-users are actively involved throughout the development lifecycle,
ensuring the end result directly aligns with their needs
•  Improved Product Quality: Built-in quality assurance, automated testing, and frequent iterations mean defects are caught 
and resolved early, ensuring a highly polished final product.
•  Reduced Risk: Frequent reviews and continuous assessment of progress allow teams to spot potential obstacles and mitigate them before they escalate
